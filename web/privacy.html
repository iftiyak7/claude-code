Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

What we store

  • On your device: everything you create in the app (amal, tasks, reminders, medicines, routines, daily progress, settings) is stored locally and the app works fully offline.
  • Only if you create an account: your email, name, a salted password hash (argon2id) and a copy of your app data, so you can back up and use multiple devices.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, share or rent your data.
  • We show no ads and use no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • Your amal and points are visible only to you — there are no public profiles or leaderboards.
  • Your location (if you allow it) is used on your device only, to calculate prayer times. It is never sent to our server.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed with argon2id; login tokens are short-lived and stored in the device's secure keystore. You can enable a biometric app lock.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from the app; this permanently erases your account and all synced data from our server. Contact: privacy@tadbeerdaily.com
